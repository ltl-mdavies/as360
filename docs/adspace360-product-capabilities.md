# Adspace360 Product Capabilities

Version: 0.1  
Last updated: 2026-06-17  
Audience: marketing, sales, executive discussions, customer conversations, product planning

## One-Line Positioning

Adspace360 is a collaborative campaign operations platform for planning, assigning, proofing, approving, and managing place-based advertising inventory from venue setup through production handoff.

## Short Description

Adspace360 helps media owners, operators, agencies, and customer administrators manage the full lifecycle of venue-based advertising campaigns. It connects venue inventory, artwork collection, creative-to-location assignment, allocation review, proof approval, transit/client approval, and project documentation into one guided workspace.

The product is designed for high-stakes, detail-heavy advertising workflows where teams need to know exactly what creative is going where, which files are approved, what still needs action, and whether downstream production teams have the right information.

## Why It Exists

Out-of-home, transit, venue, and place-based media campaigns often rely on a patchwork of spreadsheets, PDFs, shared folders, email threads, map files, and vendor portals. That creates avoidable friction:

- customers are unsure what still needs attention
- operators spend time reconciling files, specs, locations, and proof status
- multiple collaborators can accidentally work from stale information
- production and transit approval details are scattered across systems
- mobile review is painful even though users often need to act away from a desk

Adspace360 turns that scattered process into a clear operational path, while preserving the visual references and details teams need to make confident decisions.

## Who It Is For

### Customer Admins

People responsible for managing client campaigns, uploading artwork, confirming creative placements, approving proofs, and communicating project status.

### Internal Operations Teams

Teams that configure venues, maintain inventory, support customers, repair allocation issues, monitor Lift/order sync, and keep campaigns moving.

### End Clients and External Collaborators

Brand stakeholders, designers, transit reviewers, or approval contacts who need scoped access to upload files, review proof packets, approve transit creative, or view campaign materials without full admin access.

### Sales and Account Teams

Teams that need a polished, trusted campaign hub to show customers that the workflow is organized, accountable, and easy to follow.

## Core Product Promise

Adspace360 gives every stakeholder a clear, visual, and accountable view of a campaign:

- what inventory is included
- what artwork has been uploaded
- what creative is assigned to each location
- what proof files are ready or waiting
- what approvals are complete
- what documents and reports are available
- what still needs attention

## Product Pillars

### 1. Venue Inventory as a Source of Truth

Adspace360 starts with structured venue inventory instead of disconnected spreadsheets.

Capabilities:

- manage markets, venues, maps, and inventory rows
- place inventory on venue maps using normalized pin coordinates
- capture media type, dimensions, safety dimensions, status, and notes
- preserve map-specific inventory context for downstream project workflows
- support venue inventory presets/templates

Business value:

- reduces rework when creating campaigns from repeat venue inventory
- gives internal teams a consistent inventory model
- helps customers and operators understand exactly what is being sold, assigned, and approved

### 2. Inventory Presets for Faster Project Creation

Venue inventory presets let teams create curated inventory sets that can be reused across projects.

Examples:

- full venue inventory
- winter inventory
- premium-only inventory
- seasonal or event-specific inventory
- customer-specific offerings

Capabilities:

- full venue inventory exists as the default preset
- customer admins or internal users can create alternate presets
- projects can be created from a preset
- existing projects can switch inventory selection by choosing a different preset

Business value:

- speeds up project setup
- reduces mistakes from manually removing the same locations repeatedly
- makes complex venue offerings easier to package and sell

### 3. Project Dashboard

The dashboard gives users a quick command center for all active projects.

Capabilities:

- project list with status-aware rows/cards
- needs attention, awaiting proof, transit blocked, and ready/released indicators
- search and filter by project, venue, customer, focus, and mode
- smart project cards on mobile that route users to the next best action
- responsive desktop table and mobile cards

Business value:

- helps users quickly identify what needs work
- reduces time spent hunting through projects
- gives managers a fast operational snapshot

### 4. Project Hub

The Project Hub is the central workspace for a single campaign.

Capabilities:

- project summary, dates, venue, market, customer, order references, and status
- next-step guidance based on the current workflow state
- access to artwork, assignment, proof approval, transit approval, documents, share access, and activity
- mobile-specific layout that prioritizes action and orientation
- desktop workflow cards for a broader operational view

Business value:

- gives each project a single home base
- makes handoffs between workflow stages clearer
- reduces confusion around what has happened and what should happen next

### 5. Artwork Collection and Organization

The Artwork Folder organizes uploaded creative by media variant and campaign requirements.

Capabilities:

- upload artwork files
- associate artwork with media variants
- replace or delete artwork before submission when permitted
- show file metadata, thumbnails, placement status, and variant coverage
- generate/download artwork packages where supported

Business value:

- keeps creative assets tied to the correct production specs
- reduces mismatches between file, media type, and assignment
- gives customers a clear upload and review experience

### 6. Creative Assignment

Creative Assignment is the visual workspace for placing creative onto venue inventory.

Capabilities:

- map-based assignment using venue maps and inventory pins
- list-based assignment for mobile and small screens
- creative rail with upload, replace, delete, assignment count, and variant details
- assign mode for selecting locations that match a creative's media variant
- visual map pin modals with inventory specs, notes, and assignment controls
- stable-size map pins during desktop zoom and mobile pinch zoom so zooming reveals map detail without obscuring the exact placement point
- List View row actions for Details, View Map, and Clear
- Review Allocation workflow before order submission
- read-only review mode after submission

Business value:

- makes creative placement visual and auditable
- reduces assignment mistakes
- supports both desktop map workflows and mobile list workflows
- gives customers confidence before an order is submitted

### 7. Allocation Review and Downloadable Reports

Before submission, users can review the complete allocation packet. After submission, the same information remains available as a reference.

Capabilities:

- review assignment coverage by inventory and media type
- inspect which creative is assigned to each inventory location
- view allocation summary, inventory list, and map context
- download allocation/reference PDFs
- keep allocation review available after submission

Business value:

- provides a customer-friendly checkpoint before production
- creates a shared source of truth for production and installer references
- reduces disputes over what was submitted

### 8. Proof Approval

Proof Approval centralizes print proof review and approval.

Capabilities:

- sync proof lines and proof assets from Lift where configured
- queue proofs by status: all, pending, approved, revised, waiting
- review proof images and associated line details
- approve for print or upload revised artwork when permitted
- preserve proof comments, historical proof versions, and feedback context
- keep proof assets visible as read-only references even when the Lift order has moved beyond proof approval
- recognize Lift production and complete states so Dashboard and Hub can show reference, ready/released, or complete status without prompting stale Adspace actions
- mobile proof review with compact search/filter dock and next-pending navigation

Business value:

- gives customers and admins one place to approve production proofs
- reduces email-driven proof review
- keeps visual proof references available throughout the job
- supports operational accountability around approvals and revisions

### 9. Transit Approval

Transit Approval supports transit/client review workflows that may need to run alongside proofing.

Capabilities:

- review project and creative details for transit approval
- accept or reject transit creative
- record reviewer decision, notes, and status
- support reset/re-review behavior where permitted
- support scoped external access through share links

Business value:

- keeps transit/client approval separate from print proof approval
- makes approval state visible in the Project Hub and dashboard
- gives teams a cleaner way to manage external review decisions

### 10. Share Access and External Collaboration

Adspace360 supports scoped collaboration links for external participants.

Capabilities:

- create project collaboration links
- support link types such as end client collaboration, artwork upload, transit approval, and view-only access
- track participant activity and link state where persistence is configured
- revoke/regenerate links
- allow external users to participate without full internal app access

Business value:

- reduces friction for customer and end-client collaborators
- limits access to the workflow a person actually needs
- creates a more professional experience than forwarding folders and screenshots

### 11. Realtime Presence and Silent Sync

For shared operational workspaces, Adspace360 can show when multiple users are active and keep open sessions aligned.

Capabilities:

- presence indicators for shared pages such as Creative Assignment and Proof Approval
- silent sync of relevant changes across sessions
- minimal toast summaries when another user changes shared project state
- non-blocking interaction model so users can continue working

Business value:

- reduces conflict when multiple people work on the same campaign
- reassures users that the workspace is live
- supports collaborative operations without noisy interruption

### 12. Documents and Project Artifacts

The Documents area collects generated and uploaded project files.

Capabilities:

- project document repository
- generated artwork packages
- allocation reports and related project files
- upload or generate supporting artifacts where supported

Business value:

- gives teams one place to find final materials
- reduces dependency on scattered shared drives
- supports cleaner handoff to production and customer teams

### 13. Internal Allocation Repair

Allocation Override gives internal teams a controlled way to repair downstream allocation/proof/transit data when source data needs adjustment.

Capabilities:

- inspect proof-backed allocation rows
- create override rows
- assign or reassign inventory
- replace display artwork for downstream outputs
- hide rows from downstream reports
- preserve source context and audit intent

Business value:

- helps operations recover from late-stage changes or source-system mismatches
- avoids forcing customer-visible workflows to carry broken data
- protects downstream reports and approvals from known source issues

### 14. Admin Settings and Operational Configuration

Adspace360 includes administration tools for customer and system configuration.

Capabilities:

- customer settings
- notification toggles and templates
- workflow policy settings
- share-link defaults
- Lift/proof integration settings
- vendor routing concepts
- venue and customer management
- internal health/diagnostic views

Business value:

- supports customer-specific workflow differences
- keeps integration details configurable
- gives internal teams tools to manage a growing operational platform

### 15. Vendor Workspace

Vendor Workspace gives assigned production vendors a scoped operational surface after customer/admin work has reached the vendor lane.

Capabilities:

- authenticated vendor roles separate from customer/admin roles
- assigned order dashboard
- scoped vendor order detail
- assigned-line artwork visibility
- route-aware references for LTL/Lift versus external Adspace-managed vendors
- persisted proof-line route metadata for Lift-backed versus Adspace-managed production paths
- vendor proof upload for client/admin approval
- vendor proof notes routed through the customer proof feedback acknowledgment flow
- vendor line art history with current client upload, current proof, timestamps, source, and technical details
- primary/LTL pages with Lift order data and line-level Lift references
- external vendor pages without irrelevant Lift-specific language
- vendor package download with assigned artwork and scoped manifests
- selected-line bulk production/shipping updates
- market or venue ship-to destination display
- internal vendor notes and activity

Business value:

- gives production vendors the information they need without exposing customer/admin workspaces
- prevents vendors from interfering with artwork assignment before print submission
- keeps LTL/Lift-backed workflows integrated while supporting external vendors that are not in Lift
- keeps end-client proof review vendor-neutral even when production routes differ behind the scenes
- reduces duplicate manual communication around proofs, production status, and shipping

### 16. Mobile, Tablet, Desktop, and App-Like Use

Adspace360 is designed for real-world device use, not just large desktop monitors.

Capabilities:

- mobile dashboard cards
- mobile Project Hub
- mobile Creative Assignment list workflows
- mobile Proof Approval queue/review
- iPhone/iPad home-screen support through PWA assets and manifest
- dark mode and light mode
- responsive small-laptop workspace density improvements

Business value:

- lets users review, approve, and manage work away from a desk
- improves customer confidence when sharing the app externally
- avoids mobile being an afterthought for important approval workflows

## Example Customer Story

A customer admin creates a project for a venue campaign at Penn Station. They choose a curated venue inventory preset, upload creative files by media type, assign those files to the correct inventory locations, and review the allocation packet before submitting the order.

After submission, the project moves into proofing. Proof files sync from the production workflow, and the customer reviews each proof, approves the good ones, or uploads revised artwork where needed. Transit approval can be shared separately with an external reviewer. Throughout the process, the Project Hub and Dashboard show what is complete, what is waiting, and what needs action.

If an internal operator needs to repair a late-stage allocation issue, they can use Allocation Override without asking the customer to restart the workflow. The final reports, proofs, and documents remain available as references.

## What Makes Adspace360 Different

### It Connects the Whole Campaign Path

Many tools handle files, proofing, or project tracking separately. Adspace360 links venue inventory, artwork, assignment, proofing, transit approval, and documents into one operational flow.

### It Is Visual Where Visual Decisions Matter

Creative placement and proof approval are visual tasks. Adspace360 keeps maps, thumbnails, proof images, and allocation references close to the decision.

### It Supports Real Operational Complexity

The product accounts for venue inventory, maps, media variants, safety dimensions, notes, revised proofs, post-submission locks, downstream reports, and late-stage repairs.

### It Is Built for Collaboration

The app supports admins, internal operators, customers, end clients, external uploaders, and reviewers without forcing every participant into the same access model.

### It Treats Mobile as a Real Workflow Surface

Mobile is not just a squeezed desktop view. Critical workflows have mobile-specific cards, docks, and list experiences.

## Working Assumptions

These statements are inferred from the current app and should be reviewed:

- The primary commercial audience is media owners/operators and customer admins managing venue-based or transit/place-based advertising.
- The main operational integration is Lift for submitted primary print order/proof workflows.
- External vendor production routes can be Adspace-managed without Lift line/proof references.
- Adspace360 is intended to support both internal team workflows and customer-facing workflows.
- Proof approval and transit approval may involve different external stakeholders.
- Venue inventory presets are expected to become part of the sales/package strategy, not only an internal admin convenience.
- The app should feel polished enough for direct client use, not just internal operations.

## Items to Clarify

These are areas where the current app implies a direction, but the product story would benefit from sharper language:

- Primary buyer: media owner, agency, venue operator, internal LTL/Adspace operations, or all of the above?
- Naming: should the customer-facing product be "Adspace" or "Adspace360" in marketing copy?
- Market category: campaign operations platform, OOH project management, venue media workflow, proofing/allocation platform, or something else?
- Core ROI claim: faster project launch, fewer errors, reduced admin time, better customer experience, more reliable production handoff, or all of these?
- Integration story: how much should Lift be named externally versus described as production/order workflow integration?
- Proofing positioning: is Proof Approval a feature inside Adspace360, or could it become a standalone module?
- Transit Approval positioning: is this mainly for transit media compliance, end-client approval, or both?
- Share-link promise: what level of auditability should be claimed publicly today?
- Realtime collaboration: should this be marketed now, or kept as an operational enhancement until fully hardened?
- Document repository: is Documents intended to become a full client-facing file portal?
- Reporting: should allocation reports be framed as installer packets, client review packets, internal production packets, or all three?
